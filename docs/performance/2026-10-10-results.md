## Interrupted soak — excluded from acceptance

The directory named `soak` contains an **invalid, terminated run**, not a completed
one-hour result. Its [metadata and summary](2026-10-10/interrupted-soak.json)
preserve `valid: false` and `Run terminated`. The configured measurement window
is 3,600 seconds, but each game produced only 1,124 measured ticks (about 19
minutes). A flushed footer with 336,756 records and zero drops does not establish
that the requested duration completed.

For diagnosis only, sampled RSS rose from 113.66 to 151.40 MiB; 15 clients have
reconnect samples, with maximum 44.33 ms. All sampled network histories retain
one entry of each kind per device. These partial observations do not certify
one-hour stability or reconnect coverage for all 50 clients. The October 9
completed one-hour run remains the pre-compaction baseline.
